# Brand guide

Keep the co-op looking and sounding the same everywhere: website, Facebook, signage, flyers and the collection containers.

## Name

- Full: **Island Biodiesel Co-op**
- Always "Co-op" with a capital C and a hyphen in the name. In running text, "the co-op" is fine.
- Don't abbreviate to "IBC" in public — nobody on the island will know what it means.

## Tagline

**Local fuel from local waste.**

Supporting lines: *Member-owned. Made on the island.* · *From fryer to fuel tank.*

## Logo

Files: `../website/assets/logo.svg` (vector — use for print and web) and `../facebook/profile.png` (720×720 square).

The roundel shows the sun rising over the sea with a fuel drop that is also a leaf: island, energy, renewal.

- Keep clear space around it at least ¼ of its width.
- Minimum size: 32 px on screen, 15 mm in print.
- Don't stretch, recolour, add shadows, or put it on busy photos without a solid backing.

> A local designer may want to refine this mark. If so, keep the colours below so everything else still fits.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Sea | `#0B4F6C` | Primary: headers, buttons, logo ring |
| Deep Sea Dark | `#073A50` | Footers, text on light backgrounds |
| Leaf Green | `#4C9A2A` | Secondary: highlights, numbers, icons |
| Harvest Gold | `#F2B134` | Accent: main call-to-action buttons, the sun |
| Sand | `#F6F1E7` | Background |
| Ink | `#1D2A30` | Body text |

Text on Deep Sea is white. Text on Harvest Gold is Ink (not white — white on gold is hard to read).

## Typefaces

Both free from Google Fonts.

- **Headings:** Merriweather Bold — sturdy, friendly, readable.
- **Body:** Source Sans 3 — Regular, Semibold, Bold.
- If those aren't available (e.g. in Word): Georgia for headings, Arial for body.

## Voice

We sound like **a knowledgeable neighbour**, not a corporation or an activist group.

| We are | We're not |
|---|---|
| Warm, local, plain-spoken | Corporate, jargon-heavy |
| Practical — what it means for your truck, boat, kitchen | Preachy about climate |
| Proud of members and donors | Self-congratulatory |
| Honest about limits (cold weather, older engines) | Overpromising |

Examples:

- ✅ "Run a kitchen? We'll collect your used oil for free."
- ❌ "Leverage our sustainable waste-to-energy solutions."
- ✅ "Most diesel engines run on B20 without changes — check your manual."
- ❌ "Biodiesel works in any engine!"

## Photography

Real people, real places: the pump, the collection run, donor kitchens, boats in the harbour, members at events. Natural light. Avoid stock photos of generic green fields and oil drops.

# Media control policy

**Island Biodiesel Co-op** · Adopted by the board on `[DATE]` · Review annually

This policy makes sure the co-op — not any individual — owns and controls its website, social media and public voice, and that what we say in public is accurate, consistent and safe.

---

## 1. Ownership: the co-op owns every account

Every digital asset is registered to the co-op, using a co-op email address, and listed in `ACCOUNTS_REGISTER.csv`.

| Asset | Must be owned/registered by | Minimum people with full control |
|---|---|---|
| Website domain (`[YOUR-DOMAIN]`) | Co-op, with a co-op billing card or account | 2 |
| Website hosting | Co-op account | 2 |
| Co-op email (`info@`, `media@`) | Co-op account | 2 |
| Facebook Page + Meta Business portfolio | Co-op Business portfolio | 2 |
| Any other social accounts (Instagram, etc.) | Co-op email, linked to the Business portfolio where possible | 2 |
| Photo/video library | Shared co-op drive | 2 |
| Form service (website forms) | Co-op account | 2 |

Rules:

1. **No personal emails as account owners or recovery addresses.** Use `media@[YOUR-DOMAIN]` (or similar) so recovery doesn't depend on any one person.
2. **At least two full-control admins on everything**, at least one of whom is a board member.
3. **Passwords live in a shared password manager** owned by the co-op (e.g. Bitwarden Organisations, 1Password Teams) — never in email, chat, spreadsheets or this repository.
4. **Two-factor authentication is on** for every account that supports it.
5. **Domain auto-renew is on**, and the renewal date is in the board calendar. A lapsed domain is the single most common way small organisations lose their website and email.

## 2. Roles

| Role | Who | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Media lead** | Appointed by the board: `[NAME]` | Plans content, publishes, responds to comments/messages, first contact for press. |
| **Backup admin** | Board member: `[NAME]` | Full control of all accounts; covers absences; quarterly access review. |
| **Contributors** | Volunteers/staff approved by media lead | Draft posts, supply photos, reply to routine messages if given task access. No full-control access. |
| **Spokespeople** | Chair `[NAME]` and media lead | The only people who give interviews or statements on the co-op's behalf. |

## 3. What needs approval before it goes out

| Content | Approval |
|---|---|
| Routine posts from the content calendar, event reminders, thank-yous, replies to simple questions | Media lead — no further approval |
| Prices, membership terms, fuel specifications or claims about engines/emissions | Media lead **plus** board member or fuel manager checks the facts |
| Press releases, statements, anything about finances, disputes, accidents, regulators or named partners | Chair (or board delegate) |
| Paid ads / boosted posts | Within budget set by board; media lead + treasurer |

## 4. What we say — and don't

**Do**
- Stick to facts we can back up. Use the co-op's own measured numbers (litres collected, made, members).
- Credit donor kitchens and volunteers (with permission).
- Tell people to check their manufacturer's guidance on biodiesel blends.

**Don't**
- Promise that biodiesel is compatible with a specific engine or covered by a warranty.
- Quote emissions percentages without a source; if we do quote one, name the source.
- Post about members', donors' or volunteers' personal matters.
- Engage in local politics or arguments on the co-op's accounts.
- Post from the co-op's accounts while angry. Draft it, wait, get a second opinion.

## 5. Comments, messages and reviews

**Response time:** within one working day.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Question | Answer helpfully; link to the website page that covers it. |
| Fair criticism or complaint | Thank them, acknowledge, move it to private message or phone: "Sorry to hear that — could you message us so we can sort it out?" Follow up and fix. |
| Factual error | Correct politely, once, with a source. |
| Abuse, harassment, spam, personal information, hate speech | Hide or delete; ban repeat offenders. Screenshot first and log it. |
| Safety issue (spill, fire, injury, contaminated fuel) | **Don't discuss details publicly.** Go to §7. |

Do not delete comments just because they are critical.

## 6. Photos and video

- **Get permission** before posting an identifiable person. Verbal is okay for adults at events if noted; written for anything used in print, ads or the website. Use the consent line below.
- **Never post children** without written consent from a parent/guardian.
- Don't post photos showing unsafe practices (missing PPE, open methanol containers, smoking near fuel).
- Store originals in the shared co-op photo library with date, place, photographer and consent noted.
- Credit photographers when they ask.

> **Consent line:** "I agree that Island Biodiesel Co-op may use photos/video of me taken on [DATE] on its website, social media and printed materials. I can withdraw this at any time for future use." — Name, signature, date.

## 7. Crisis and incident communications

Examples: fuel spill, fire, injury, a bad fuel batch, regulator action, a serious public complaint, an account being hacked.

1. **Safety first.** Emergency services and regulators before social media.
2. **Tell the chair and media lead immediately.** Pause scheduled posts.
3. **One voice.** Only the chair or media lead speaks publicly. Everyone else: "I'll ask our chair to get back to you."
4. **Holding statement within a few hours**, approved by the chair:
   > "We're aware of [brief, factual description]. [The safety of our members and community comes first / No one was hurt.] We're looking into it and will share an update by [TIME/DATE]. Questions: [MEDIA-EMAIL]."
5. **Update when promised**, even if there's little new to say.
6. **Afterwards**, review what happened and update this policy.

**If an account is hacked:** use the backup admin to remove the intruder, reset passwords, check 2FA, and post a short notice that any odd messages weren't from us. For Facebook, use facebook.com/hacked.

## 8. When someone leaves

The same week a media lead, admin or contributor steps down:

- [ ] Remove their access from every account in the register
- [ ] Change any shared passwords they knew
- [ ] Hand over drafts, photo files and the content calendar
- [ ] Update `ACCOUNTS_REGISTER.csv` and the roles table above

## 9. Quarterly checklist (board agenda item)

- [ ] Review who has access to each account; remove anyone who shouldn't
- [ ] Confirm 2FA is still on for all admins
- [ ] Check domain and hosting renewal dates
- [ ] Check the website's contact details, hours and prices still match Facebook
- [ ] Glance at Facebook Insights: reach, followers, top posts

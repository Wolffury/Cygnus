## Story ideas to pitch to local media

- First 10,000 litres of local biodiesel — a milestone story with photos at the pump
- A day on the collection run with a volunteer
- A local fishing boat or farm that runs on co-op fuel
- Open day / tours for schools
- What islanders pay to ship fuel in vs. what stays local (with sourced figures)

# Facebook content calendar — first 4 weeks after the refresh

Three posts a week (Tue / Thu / Sat works well for local audiences). Every post is ready to copy; fill in the `[BRACKETS]` and add a photo. **Real photos of real people and places beat graphics every time** — see the photo rules in `../media-kit/MEDIA_CONTROL_POLICY.md` §6 before posting anyone's face.

Content mix to aim for over time:

| Type | Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Community & people (donor kitchens, members, volunteers) | 40% | Builds the relationships that keep oil coming in |
| How-it-works & fuel tips | 25% | Builds trust in the fuel |
| Asks (join, donate oil, volunteer, events) | 20% | Drives action |
| Milestones & impact numbers | 15% | Shows progress |

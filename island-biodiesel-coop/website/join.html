How a co-op works

A cooperative is owned and democratically controlled by its members. Every member gets one vote regardless of how much they buy. Members elect the board, approve the budget, and decide what happens with any surplus.

Our members also volunteer on collection days, at events, and in the shop — but there's no obligation to.

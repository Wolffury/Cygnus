Why an island biodiesel co-op?

🌊

Less fuel shipped in

Every litre we make here is a litre that doesn't have to cross the water by barge or tanker. That's money and resilience that stay on the island.

♻️

Waste becomes a resource

Fryer oil that used to be trucked off-island, dumped, or poured down drains now powers boats, trucks, generators and farm machinery.

🤝

Owned by the people who use it

One member, one vote. Surplus goes back to members and into the community — not to shareholders somewhere else.

From fryer to fuel tank

  1. Collect. We pick up used cooking oil from restaurants, cafés, schools and households on a regular route.
  2. Clean. Oil is settled, filtered and dewatered to remove food particles and moisture.
  3. Convert. Through transesterification, the oil becomes biodiesel. The by-product, glycerin, is put to use rather than wasted.
  4. Test. Each batch is washed, dried and checked for quality before it leaves the shop.
  5. Fuel up. Members buy biodiesel at our pump for vehicles, boats, generators and heating.

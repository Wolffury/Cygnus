Donate your used cooking oil

Free collection for restaurants, cafés, food trucks, schools and events — plus a drop-off point for households.

For businesses

  1. Fill in the form below or call us.
  2. We drop off a sealed, labelled collection container sized to your volume.
  3. Pour cooled oil in after service. Keep out water, food scraps and cleaning products.
  4. We collect on a regular schedule — weekly, fortnightly, or when you call.

You get a quarterly certificate showing how much oil you diverted — great for your customers and your sustainability reporting.

For households

Let oil cool, strain it back into its original bottle or any sealed plastic container, and bring it to our drop-off point:

When: [DAYS & HOURS]

We can take

Vegetable, canola, sunflower, peanut, soybean and other cooking oils; frying fat.

Please don't bring

Motor oil, mineral oil, grease-trap waste, or oil mixed with water or chemicals.

Order emails

One email for each supplier, written from the quote with the delivery dates from your timeline. Put in the supplier's address, check the email, then tap Open in email app and press Send there.

This address goes in the signature. The email itself sends from the account set up in your email app, so add this address to the app first (Android: Gmail app › Add another account › Other; PC: Outlook or Mail › Add account). If the app opens on a different account, tap the From line and switch.

Site

Measurements: type 4' 6", 4ft 6in, 4.5', 54" or 1.37m. A plain number means inches.

Sync between devices

Sync works when the planner is open in the Claude app or claude.ai. Every device signed in to your Claude account sees the same job, saved jobs, prices, materials and company details, and changes show up on the others within a few seconds.

Your photos

The picture is drawn by the planner to the published size, in the colour you pick. Real product colours vary by batch; check samples with your supplier. "See photos online" opens the real product pictures in your browser. To keep one here, save it there and tap Add a photo; your photos show here on all your devices.

My jobs

Every plan you've saved. Opening one saves the job you have open first, so nothing is lost. Saved jobs sync between your devices.
